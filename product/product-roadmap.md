# Product Roadmap

## Sections

### 1. Discovery Radar
The home screen — a real-time proximity radar showing nearby Gravity members ranked by match score. Tapping a member opens the lightweight intro pane.

### 2. Profile
The user's own profile: avatar, headline, interests, current intent, and privacy mode toggle. Edits propagate instantly to nearby members' radars.

### 3. Connections
The chronological feed of people the user has messaged, been messaged by, or explicitly saved. Shows last-seen-near hints and surfaces stale connections that haven't been pinged in a while.

### 4. Messages
Lightweight, 1:1 only. No group chats. Per-thread "still nearby?" indicator and templated openers seeded from the matched dimension.

### 5. Settings & Privacy
Privacy mode picker, location accuracy preferences, push notification toggles, account management, and the Event Mode shortcut.
